What Fleasion can and cannot change

Fleasion cannot clear all Android RAM, force a Roblox-only screen resolution, or promise a 5–10% FPS gain. Display settings affect the whole phone; Roblox graphics settings control in-game render load. No system processes are killed and thermal protections remain on.
